## Brand & Style
This design system defines an executive-grade privacy and vocal shielding platform engineered for high-profile individuals, board members, and dignitaries. The emotional posture is quiet, impenetrable assurance: it rejects chaotic, alarmist cybersecurity aesthetics (such as flickering terminal logs, red alert flashing, and dense noisy telemetry matrices) in favor of serene architectural composure.

The visual direction merges **Minimalism** with **Bespoke Luxury Skeuomorphic Restraint**. It emphasizes deep obsidian negative space, precision hairline structural borders, muted champagne-gold accents, and delicate micro-lighting. The user experience conveys the serenity of a private acoustic sanctuary—discreet, effortless, and decisively protective.

## Colors
The palette evokes private vaults, brushed precious metals, and high-frequency audio shielding:

- **Obsidian Voids (Base Surfaces)**:
  - Base Obsidian: `#090B10` (Root viewport canvas, deepest black-blue)
  - Elevated Obsidian: `#0E121B` (Structural cards, elevated drawers, floating panels)
  - Surface Highlight: `#141A26` (Hover surfaces, activated tile states)
- **Subtle Graphite (Structural Outlines & Separators)**:
  - Perimeter Hairline: `#1C2230` (Ghost borders, panel dividers)
  - Defined Focus Border: `#252D3D` (Interactive bounds, active state frames)
- **Champagne & Auric Accents (Primary Sovereignty & Assurance)**:
  - Champagne Gold Primary: `#E5C365` (Primary CTAs, shield status active, premium locks)
  - Deep Burnished Gold: `#D4AF37` (Muted badges, luxury toggle tracks)
  - Pale Champagne Mist: `#F5E6BE` (High-contrast typography accents, focal highlights)
- **Electric Royal Cyan (Acoustic Shielding & Real-Time Pulse)**:
  - Sovereign Cyan: `#38BDF8` (Active voice encryption waves, zero-latency indicators, real-time node confirmation)
- **Neutral Monochromes**:
  - Primary Text: `#F8FAFC`
  - Secondary / Muted Text: `#94A3B8`
  - Disabled / Whispered Text: `#475569`

## Typography
Typographic hierarchy relies exclusively on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, utilizing its geometric purity and open counters to project transparency and architectural balance. 

- **Weight Discipline**: Restrict headline weights to `500` and `600`. Heavy weights (`800+`) are strictly avoided to eliminate visual bulk.
- **Micro-Labels**: Security indicators, cryptographic states, and biometric metadata use `label-caps` with uppercase transformation and a generous `0.12em` tracking, providing an elite horological or instrumentation aesthetic.
- **Optical Density**: Line heights are spaced generously to prevent cognitive fatigue during sensitive operations.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a disciplined fixed-max container model set against an unbounded obsidian backdrop.

- **Grid Architecture**: 
  - **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column grid with a maximum content boundary of `1240px`, `1.5rem` gutters, and `3rem` margins.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column layout with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` safe margins.
  - **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` safe outer margins.
- **Rhythm & Whitespace**: Spacing is intentionally oversized. Key modules must float within ample empty space (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) to establish an executive-suite atmosphere where components are never compressed.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is achieved through **Tonal Stratification and Inner Light** rather than aggressive drop shadows.

- **Base to Elevated**: The background sits at `#090B10`. Structural panels rise to `#0E121B` with a dual-boundary technique:
  - **Outer Border**: 1px solid `#1C2230`.
  - **Inner Light Rim**: 1px inset border `inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
- **Frosted Veil (Backdrop Blur)**: Floating controls, modal sheets, and audio intercept panels utilize `rgba(14, 18, 27, 0.72)` paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(160%)`.
- **Luminescence (Champagne & Cyan Halos)**: Active voice shielding shields discard hard drop shadows in favor of a soft, wide-radius bloom: `0 0 40px -10px rgba(229, 195, 101, 0.15)` for gold status, and `0 0 32px -8px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.18)` for real-time acoustic obfuscation.

## Shapes
The shape philosophy balances high-tech precision with bespoke comfort. Base interactive controls, metric tiles, and floating sheets apply a `0.5rem` (8px) radius, while larger defensive cards and acoustic isolation containers scale to `1rem` (16px) or `1.5rem` (24px).

Micro badges, mute toggles, and voice pulse rings utilize absolute circular geometry (`rounded-full`) to contrast against the structured architectural tiles.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Sovereign**: Solid `#E5C365` background with dark obsidian text (`#090B10`, font-weight 600). Hairline border in `#F5E6BE` at 20% opacity. Subtle golden hover lift with zero hard drop shadow.
- **Secondary / Stealth**: Dark translucent core (`rgba(20, 26, 38, 0.8)`), border in `#252D3D`, text in `#F8FAFC`. On hover, border transitions smoothly to `#E5C365` with a subtle inner gold sheen.
- **Ghost / Quiet Dismiss**: Transparent background, text in `#94A3B8`, transitioning to `#F5E6BE` on hover with zero physical motion.

### 2. Cards & Acoustic Enclosures
- Container surface set to `#0E121B` with subtle 1px border `#1C2230`.
- Integrated top-edge specular highlight using `inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(245, 230, 190, 0.08)`.
- High inner padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) keeping telemetry data uncluttered.

### 3. Chips & Cryptographic Status Tags
- Pill-shaped or subtle 6px corner radius.
- Background `rgba(28, 34, 48, 0.5)` with 1px border in `#252D3D`.
- Text in `label-caps` uppercase styling with a micro 6px glowing status node (Champagne `#E5C365` for armed state, Electric Cyan `#38BDF8` for real-time acoustic encryption).

### 4. Input Fields
- Monolithic `#090B10` background framed by a `#1C2230` border.
- Inset padding `0.875rem 1rem`.
- Focus state drops standard blue browser rings, employing instead an unbroken `#E5C365` border with an ultra-soft gold ambient flare (`0 0 16px rgba(229, 195, 101, 0.12)`).

### 5. Checkboxes & Switches
- **Switches**: Track in `#1C2230` (36px × 20px). When active, track transitions to deep gold `#D4AF37`. The thumb is finished in `#F5E6BE` with a slight mechanical drop shadow.
- **Checkboxes**: Square with 4px border radius. Unchecked in `#1C2230`. Checked state filled with `#E5C365`, featuring an obsidian micro-check mark.

### 6. Acoustic Shield Indicators (Specialized Product Component)
- Replaces standard dense telemetry graphs with a single minimal harmonic sine wave or concentric resonance ring.
- Lines rendered with ultra-fine 1.5px stroke in `#38BDF8` fading into `#0E121B` edges.
- Unobtrusive numerical indicators (e.g., "99.98% OBFUSCATION") positioned with `label-caps` in `#94A3B8`.
